## Long paragraphs for quotes

Select a few words in any paragraph and a copy link button shows up. Select across two paragraphs, or start or end in the middle of a word, to try the harder cases. A selection that starts or ends halfway through a word leaves that word out, because links only match whole words.

This is a long paragraph to select from. It goes on for a while so there are plenty of words to pick, at the start, in the middle and at the end. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, then the lazy dog wakes up, stretches, and goes back to sleep in the sun. Somewhere a cat watches all of it and does nothing, which is the most cat thing to do.

Another long paragraph, right after the first one, to select across both. It talks about nothing in particular. Rainy afternoons are good for reading, sunny mornings are good for walks, and late nights are good for sleeping, even if nobody actually does that. Tomorrow is another day.
